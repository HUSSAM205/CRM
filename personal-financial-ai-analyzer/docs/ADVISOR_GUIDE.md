# Advisor guide (for users)

The Advisor (page **Financial advisor**) turns your validated data into transparent planning help. It does **not** give investment, credit, tax or legal advice. The rules are in `ADVISOR.md`.

## Facts only you know

These are optional and kept only in this session:
* current balance and current savings;
* monthly income;
* monthly obligations that are not in the file.

Anything you leave empty stays **unknown**. The app never infers a bank balance from transactions.

## What you can do

| Tab or section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Financial profile | average monthly inflows and expenses, split into essential, non-essential and unclassified; recurring payment candidates; surplus or deficit. The basis is stated |
| Recommendations | Ranked, each with its reason, evidence, calculation, assumptions, expected monthly and annual impact, and evidence sufficiency. Essential categories are never suggested for cuts |
| Savings goals | create and edit a goal (target, already saved, monthly contribution, target month). Shows required monthly saving, completion month, milestones at 25/50/75/100% and status |
| Emergency fund | you choose the coverage in months and the basis (essential or all expenses). The calculation and its assumptions are shown |
| What-if scenarios | change a category, all expenses, income or monthly saving; add a one-off or recurring cost. Labelled "Simulation, not a prediction", with the effect on goals |
| Can I afford this? | Without a known balance **and** known obligations the answer is always "Cannot confirm affordability". You still see the measurable effect |

You can also ask the assistant: "How much can I save?", "Any recommendations?", "What if I reduce dining by 10%?", "Can I afford 450?"
