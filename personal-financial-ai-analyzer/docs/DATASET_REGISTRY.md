## Rejected or unused candidates (searched 2026-11-27)

Master audit 3 re-checked the five named candidates; see `reports/master_audit_3/DATASET_CANDIDATES.md`. Dataset A and Dataset B were re-verified live: hashes and revision PASS. No new dataset was adopted.

| Candidate | Why it was not used |
|---|---|
| Kaggle-hosted personal-finance and fraud sets (e.g. "Sparkov", ULB credit-card fraud) | Access requires a Kaggle login (an access restriction); mirrors elsewhere have unclear provenance |
| OpenML 43627 (ULB credit-card fraud mirror) | Features are anonymised PCA components, with no account history or categories, so it cannot feed the app's account-relative features. Corporate card data from 2013, not personal budgeting |
| UCI Wholesale customers (CC BY 4.0) | Business clients' annual totals; not personal transactions |
| BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey | Household survey aggregates, not transaction-level account histories; aggregate economic indicators are not individual records |
| IEEE DataPort "daily expense prediction" | Access restricted to subscribers; licence unclear |
| Other HF personal-finance sets (Finance-Agentic-AI, infinite-dataset-hub, user uploads) | Small, LLM-generated or unclear provenance and licence; no added value over B |

## Data-quality summary

* **A:** see `reports/final_master_audit/DATA_QUALITY_REPORT.json`. Highlights: 0 nulls, 0 duplicates, all 50M rows reconciled, intermittent monthly series (45–63% zero months).
* **B** (`reports/experiments/dataset_b/quality.json`):
  * 0 nulls in 6 columns;
  * 0 unparseable timestamps;
  * 1,839,209 negative amounts, of which 1,839,137 are card payments (type `payment`) and the rest are refunds or other credits;
  * 1,105 zero amounts;
  * 23,991 free-text merchant types (LLM-generated);
  * 17,668 users with at least 12 active months;
  * 0% zero months within each user's span;
  * each user's first and last month is partial (excluded from E2-B targets);
  * within-user lag-1 autocorrelation is −0.048.
* **C:** generated exactly to specification; 0 validation rejections.
