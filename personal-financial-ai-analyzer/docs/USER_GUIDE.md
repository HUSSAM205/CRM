# User Guide

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Transactions** | Upload a CSV (or load the demo). Confirm the column mapping: Date/time, Amount and Currency are required. If there is no status column, confirm the rows are completed. If there is no type column, negative amounts count as spending and positive amounts as money received. Rejected rows are listed with reasons. Filter by category, type and status. |
| **Overview** | Income, expenses, net cash flow and transfers for the chosen account, currency and period, with a monthly chart. Download the monthly report as CSV. |
| **Analytics** | Tabs: categories and a two-month comparison; spending per day, week or month with average, median, highest and lowest month; recorded inflows and monthly cash flow (not a bank balance); recurring payment candidates and changes in category spending; transparent indicators (no combined score). |
| **AI predictions** | Next-month **spending outlook** (typical month, your usual range, a benchmark-calibrated 80% interval; needs at least 12 active months). Machine-learning forecasting was not effective on the evaluation data, and the page says so. **Unusual transactions — review recommended**: the top 5, with the usual amount and an explanation. Model comparison and limitations are shown too. |
| **Budget** | Suggested budgets (the median of the previous three months). Double-click a budget cell to edit it. Warnings appear at 80% and 100%. |
| **Financial advisor** | Optionally enter facts only you know (balance, savings, income, obligations). See ranked recommendations with evidence and calculations, add savings goals (required monthly amount, completion month, feasibility), run what-if scenarios (labelled simulations), and check whether a purchase is affordable (never confirmed without a known balance). |
| **Financial assistant** | Ask: "How much did I spend in March?", "How much did I spend on groceries?", "Compare January and February.", "Am I exceeding my budget?", "What is my expected spending next month?", "What was my cash flow last month?", "Is my spending increasing?", "Show unusual transactions", "Am I on track with my goals?", "Any recommendations?", "What if I reduce dining by 10%?", "Can I afford 450?". Questions about fraud, investments, loans or tax, and anything else, get a fixed "not supported" reply. |
| **Settings and privacy** | Choose the retention period, see what the app holds, delete everything, and read how your data is handled. |

**Privacy.** Data stays in your browser session on your own computer. Choose how long it is kept in the sidebar, or press **Delete my data now**. Amounts in different currencies are never added together.

**Not advice.** This is an educational prototype. Forecasts are estimates and anomalies are not fraud findings.
