## Not a conflict, but interpretation needed

* **Fees** (HW2 5.2 lists `fee` as its own meaning): fees are reported as a separate outflow line. They count in *total expenses / net cash flow* but are kept out of *category spending* totals, so category budgets reflect merchant spending. See `FINANCIAL_RULES.md`.
* **Interest**: the HWs do not name a meaning for it. The direction is **measured** from `balance_after` (see `FINANCIAL_RULES.md`), and it is classed as an inflow, not salary.
* **"Precision@k"** (HW2 Table 8.2): k is not defined in HW2. k = the number of injected anomalies in the evaluated set, so Precision@k equals Recall@k. That choice is documented and both are reported. Precision@{50,100} is also reported.
